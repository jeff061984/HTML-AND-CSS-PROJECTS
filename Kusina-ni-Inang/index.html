Inang's Special Dishes

Three classic Filipino dishes for the whole family.

SOUP · MAIN DISH

Pork Sinigang

A savory and sour soup with tender pork and fresh vegetables.

Ingredients

Pork, tamarind or sinigang mix, radish, string beans, water spinach, tomatoes, and onions.

Instructions

Boil the pork until tender. Add the souring ingredient and vegetables, then cook until done.

VEGETABLES · MAIN DISH

Chopsuey

A colorful mix of crisp vegetables with chicken or shrimp.

Ingredients

Cabbage, carrots, chayote, bell peppers, cauliflower, chicken or shrimp, garlic, and soy sauce.

Instructions

Sauté the garlic and meat. Add the harder vegetables, a little water, and seasonings. Add the remaining vegetables and cook until tender.

TRADITIONAL FILIPINO DISH

Pinakbet

A delicious mix of vegetables cooked with fermented shrimp paste.

Ingredients

Squash, eggplant, bitter melon, string beans, okra, tomatoes, onions, and shrimp paste.

Instructions

Sauté the tomatoes and onions. Add the vegetables and a little water. Season with shrimp paste and simmer until the vegetables are cooked.
